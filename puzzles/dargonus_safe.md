## Context

This puzzle is part of the "How Did It All End?" secret ending chain. The Theodora dataslate retrieved here is one of several required items. Miss it before the Ch2->Ch3 PoNR and the secret ending is permanently locked.

## Hint Ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** In the palace bedroom, interact with the bookshelf. The puzzle involves selecting answers from books and music clues in the room.

**Lvl 2 (more):** Three answers are required in sequence. Look at the books on the shelves and listen for or examine the music-related objects in the bedroom -- they encode the combination.

**Lvl 3 (full solution):** Reach the saferoom first: in your quarters in the von Valancius passage, interact with the book on the nightstand to open Theodora's treasure room, go in, and **sit on the sofa under the painting**. The puzzle is three selections, and these are the correct ones:

| Prompt | Correct answer |
|---|---|
| Wine | **Flame of Purity** |
| Book | **Litanies of the Motive Force** |
| Music | **The Symphony of Honour and Duty, Part 2** |

Note the spelling: the game uses **Honour**, and the music choice specifies **Part 2** -- one widely-copied guide drops both, so a list that reads "Symphony of Honor and Duty" is quoting a less careful source.

## Reward

Unlocks the **Data-Bank / von Valancius Coat of Arms** quest item -- one of the two data repositories needed to decrypt Theodora's encrypted databank, and one of multiple required items for the "How Did It All End?" secret ending. There is no separate loot reward; the payoff is the quest chain.
