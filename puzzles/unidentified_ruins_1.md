## Hint Ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** This is a beam-pattern puzzle. Look at the geometric markings on the floor around the central pillar -- they suggest a specific orientation pattern.

**Lvl 2 (more):** The beams need to align in a pentagonal pattern. The floor markings show you the target configuration.

**Lvl 3 (full solution):** At the central pillar (requires Lore (Warp) +20), arrange the beams in a **pentagonal pattern** matching the ground markings. No specific left/right sequence documented -- the pattern is readable from the floor geometry.
