# Puzzle -- Dreams and Stories (Footfall Hidden Caches)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-05-22

**puzzle-tier-required:** 3 (full solution)

**Zone:** z-foot-dock / z-foot-atrium / z-foot-shadow (Footfall -- multiple areas)
**Type:** cache hunt + keyword/cogitator entry
**Chapter:** 2 (ONLY -- Shadow Quarters closes Ch3+)
**Missable:** YES -- hard missable; Shadow Quarters is removed from the fast-travel map entirely by Ch4

## Prerequisites

1. Talk to Skatov (NPC in Footfall area) to start the "Dreams and Stories" errand.
2. Complete *Reclaim What Was Lost* (Kiava Gamma main quest) FIRST to unlock access to the Shadow Quarters (edge E06 in `nav/architecture.md`).
3. **Bug warning (full-Dogmatic-incognito + refused food shipment):** if playing Dogmatic and refused the food shipment, the quest may get permanently stuck on "Wait for situation to resolve itself" -- also ruins the Dreams errand and locks you out of the Righteous Fury Cape. Avoid this combination.

## Hint Ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** There are 4 hidden caches scattered across Footfall's different areas. Find them all, then use the founder-statue cogitator in the Atrium.

**Lvl 2 (more):** Collect all 4 caches (in Atrium, Alpha-Rho/Void Dock, and Shadow Quarters), then at the cogitator you'll need to enter a specific keyword derived from the cache contents.

**Lvl 3 (full solution):** Collect all 4 caches. At the Atrium founder-statue cogitator, enter the keyword **"realizing design"** (exact phrase, must match). This unlocks the reward cache.

## Reward

- **Focus Incense**
- **Righteous Fury Cape**
