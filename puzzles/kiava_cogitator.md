## Context

This is part of the "How Did It All End?" secret ending chain. The Theodora dataslate found here is one of several required items. The bridge cogitator hack (interact with bridge cogitator at Ch1 end) is a separate step in the same chain.

## Hint Ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** Look for a concealed passage or gear mechanism before reaching the final boss in this area. The environment has mechanical elements that can be manipulated.

**Lvl 2 (more):** There's a brake mechanism that can be removed, which then lets you take a gear. The gear goes into a cogitator, but the path to the cogitator has traps.

**Lvl 3 (full solution):**

*Getting the gear:*
1. Follow the rail line **south**. Behind the transport is a second wagon on the same line.
2. An obstacle blocks it -- pass an **Athletics** test to clear it.
3. Interact with the **cogitator between the wagons** to release the brakes, which frees the gear.

*Reaching the sealed room (eastern portion of the dungeon):*
4. Head to the **eastern** part of the zone and interact with the computer there.
5. Choose **"Bring your electoo to the scanner"** for a short scene.
6. Interact with the **goods** beside the wall and place the **valve** on it -- the valve sits under the **Notable** section of your inventory.

*The room itself -- ORDER MATTERS:*
7. Enter slowly and **disable both traps BEFORE touching the computer.** There are exactly two.
8. The computer then offers two options. **Turn off the gas supply FIRST**, then request Theodora's data. Requesting the data first floods the room you are standing in with gas.

**Terminology note (2026-09-17):** the P1 research described a single "gear -> cogitator" insertion; the walkthrough sources describe a brake-release that frees a **gear** AND a separate **valve** placed on the goods by the wall. These may be two steps in one chain or two names for one part -- recorded as found rather than smoothed into agreement. Verify on the next live run.

## Reward
