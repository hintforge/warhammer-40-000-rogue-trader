## Context

This is part of the "How Did It All End?" secret ending chain. The Theodora dataslate found here is one of several required items. The bridge cogitator hack (interact with bridge cogitator at Ch1 end) is a separate step in the same chain.

## Hint Ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** Look for a concealed passage or gear mechanism before reaching the final boss in this area. The environment has mechanical elements that can be manipulated.

**Lvl 2 (more):** There's a brake mechanism that can be removed, which then lets you take a gear. The gear goes into a cogitator, but the path to the cogitator has traps.

**Lvl 3 (full solution):** 
1. Find and **remove the brake** on the mechanical assembly.
2. **Take the gear** that is released.
3. Navigate to the cogitator while avoiding traps.
4. **Insert the gear into the cogitator** -- this opens the hidden passage and retrieves the Theodora dataslate.
