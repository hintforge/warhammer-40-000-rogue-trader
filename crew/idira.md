## Archetype

Unsanctioned Psyker origin -> various Tier II paths.

## Key mechanic

**Flat 5% Perils floor:** Idira has a permanent 5% Perils of the Warp chance on ANY psychic power, irrespective of Veil Degradation level. This floor has NOT been removed as of Patch 1.5 (community-tested). The Astropathic Amulet (Act 1) blocks Horrors-of-the-Warp debuffs but does NOT block this Perils floor.

Starting stat advantage: +1 Psy Rating at game start vs. Sanctioned Psykers.

## Managing Idira's peril risk

Community-tested management strategies:
- **Cassia pairing:** Cassia's Navigator powers actively reduce VD each use, counteracting Idira's contribution to the global VD meter.
- **Stabilizing Factor talent:** Idira has a talent that reduces her own peril proc chance.
- **Overseer Psyber Raven path (LI DLC):** If Idira is built as an Overseer, perils can be channeled through the Psyber Raven familiar onto enemies instead of the party. Largely eliminates peril risk for that build path. `spoiler: dlc:lex-imperialis`
- **Intentional high-VD builds:** On lower difficulties, intentionally letting perils trigger can be viable (peril effects on enemies can be powerful).
