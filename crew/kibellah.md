### Death Cult Assassin origin talents (RESOLVED 2026-06-29)
The old P1 names were CORRECT -- they 404'd only because Fextralife gives companion talents no standalone pages; their effects live as rows on the master `/Talents` list. These are a **blood-cult stacking engine**: they pile `bloodblessed`/`bloodstained` stacks on enemies and reward fighting on blood-covered cells. The InEffect guide skips them ("too slow") -- they reward a dedicated blood/DoT build, not the Executioner burst line InEffect runs.

| Talent | Effect | Build value |
|---|---|---|
| Crimson Tide | A bloodblessed/bloodstained creature gains +1 stack whenever Raining Blood is re-inflicted on it OR a turn starts on a blood-covered cell | Engine core -- only if going blood build |
| Dance of Blood | Kibellah's allies deal +(2 + AGI bonus)% damage to enemies when BOTH the ally and the target stand on blood-covered cells | Party blood-build payoff |
| Dance of Thorns | Each time Kibellah parries or dodges, all `bloodstained` enemies gain +1 stack | Synergises with her high dodge/parry |
| Open the Veins | A bloodblessed/bloodstained creature gains +1 stack whenever bleed, burning, or toxin is inflicted on it | Pairs with Executioner DoT -- but InEffect still skips |
| Wounds Streaming Blood | A bloodblessed/bloodstained creature gains +1 stack whenever it takes damage from bleed/burning/toxin | Same -- DoT-build only |
| Raining Blood | Her next melee attack makes the target explode in a burst of blood, covering the floor and enemies behind it; affected enemies take additional damage. (She also has a throwable "blood phial" to spill more blood.) Blood-covered cells are what the talents above key off | Engine trigger -- game-file confirmed |

**Verdict:** These are a real, coherent build path (blood-cult attrition), but they pay off slowly and want a committed bleed/blood-cell build. InEffect's Bladedancer->Executioner->Exemplar line ignores them in favour of burst DoT; follow that unless deliberately building blood-cult. Full origin entry: [`../items/talents-origin.md`](../items/talents-origin.md).
