Aeldari Ranger companion. Two recruitment-gate points; permanent removal triggers documented.

## Recruitment gate 1 (z-janus, Chapter 2)

- Do NOT attack the Aeldari during the **first two dialogue replies** at the Webway Gateway confrontation in z-janus
- Attacking during those replies removes Yrliet permanently for the run (Fextralife / gamerguides)
- Also: visit the Janus gazebo before completing World Shapers to trigger Aeldari in Distress (missable Ch.2 window)

## Recruitment gate 2 (z-chasm, Chapter 3)

- Location: Crossroads in z-chasm (Streets of the Chasm)
- Action: Rescue / recruit Yrliet at the crossroads; killing or letting her die permanently removes her
- Required for z-webway progression: Yrliet must be in the **active party** throughout the Spire arc -- Owlcat-confirmed bug blocks the elevator without her (roguetrader.owlcat.games/news/en/33)

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-013: z-webway elevator soft-lock if Yrliet is benched; documented independently in `nav/z-webway.md` Soft-lock warnings.
