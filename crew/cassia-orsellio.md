## Combat role (community-observed)

**Primary tool: force-move, not Warp Gaze.** Cassia's force-move ability (psychic displacement of enemies to a target location) is community-rated more impactful than her Warp Gaze direct damage. Clustering enemies enables mass kills via attacks of opportunity (AoO) and AoE follow-up, often clearing large portions of a map before other characters take a turn.

**Core combo with Pasqal:** Pasqal has an axe that triggers an AoE attack on every attack of opportunity. Combined with Cassia's force-move clustering enemies into his melee threat range, this generates mass AoE without Pasqal spending AP directly. Axe name not confirmed in community threads; cross-reference Pasqal melee talent tree.

**Grand Strategist + Cassia:** Both the RT and Cassia running Grand Strategist produces overwhelming action economy. Community rates two Grand Strategists as trivializing combat on any difficulty -- field at most one.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-010: This pairing is independently documented in mechanics.md Combat System (Origins section).

**[BUG present Patch 1.5] Turn reset:** Cassia occasionally resets her full turn (all AP and cooldowns restored) when dropping Grand Strategy zones. Observed at up to 3 full turns in a single combat; triggers fairly consistently. Whether intentional is unconfirmed.
