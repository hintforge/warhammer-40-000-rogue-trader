## Arch-Militant (T2)
_stat focus: WS + BS, AGI, STR_

**Patch 1.2.0.25 nerf (June 26 2024):** "Several of Arch-Militant's abilities and talents were changed to give bigger bonuses at the start of the combat at the cost of reducing their scaling from a number of Versatility stacks ... List of abilities and talents impacted: Versatility, Confident Approach, Adaptability, Critical Versatility, Dependable." Same patch significantly buffed Pyromancy Ignite (minimum damage raised to 1+WP Bonus+6x Psy Rating from 1+5x Psy Rating), driving the meta shift away from Arch-Militant toward Bounty Hunter/Master Tactician/Pyromancer builds. Pre-1.2 guides overstate Arch-Militant damage.
