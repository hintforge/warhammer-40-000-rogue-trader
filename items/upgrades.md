## Three-Tier Archetype System

One archetype per tier. Tier 2 is constrained by Tier 1 choice (each Tier 1 grants access to ~3 Tier 2 options). Exemplar (Tier 3) draws capstone talents from both prior archetypes.

Stats and skills upgrade twice per tier (+5 per stat, +10 per skill); 8 free ability picks across full progression.

### Tier 1 (Levels 1-15)

| Archetype | Focus | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Melee frontline | Best Tier 2 pairings: Assassin (mobile DPS), Vanguard (tank), Arch-Militant (hybrid) |
| Officer | Support; extra-turn economy | Converged early recommendation; no second Officer until Cassia joins Ch1 end |
| Soldier | Ranged DPS | Dominant pairing: Arch-Militant for sustained ranged |
| Operative | Skill coverage; debuffs | Bounty Hunter is the primary Tier 2 pairing; Pasqal templates this |

### Tier 2 (Levels 16-35)

| Archetype | Focus | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| Assassin | Mobile melee DPS; dodge | Death Waltz ability chain; high AGI scaling |
| Arch-Militant | Sustained DPS; versatility | Dominant Ch2+ ranged build (Soldier -> Arch-Militant + calibrated heavy stubber) |
| Bounty Hunter | Sniper-utility; debuffs | Operative -> Bounty Hunter; Drukhari sniper rifle Ch3 |
| Grand Strategist | Zone control + extra turns | Officer synergy |
| Master Tactician | Momentum AoE | Officer or Soldier |
| Vanguard | Tank; frontline | Warrior -> Vanguard for physical defense |
| Overseer | Buff / command | Available to Officer/Operative or psyker Origins; DLC status disputed (see limitations.md) |

### Tier 3 / Exemplar (Levels 36-45)

Universal shared talent pool (no archetype-locked Exemplar talents); plus re-pick slots from T1 and T2 trees. Full talent list and path-specific pick recommendations in [`talents-exemplar.md`](talents-exemplar.md).

### DLC Archetypes (out of P1 scope)

- **Bladedancer** (Tier 1, Void Shadows) + **Executioner** (Tier 2, Void Shadows) -- melee-mobile psyker endgame peak.
- Arbitrator sub-paths (Lex Imperialis) -- out of P1 scope.
