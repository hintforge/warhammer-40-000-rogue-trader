# Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader -- Homeworld Talents

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-29
**research_run:** doctor 2026-06-29 (Firecrawl)

Every homeworld grants a fixed set of ~5 talents plus a stat profile and a Feature. These talents feed the **"Available Common Talents"** screen at level-up alongside [origin talents](talents-origin.md) and the [universal common pool](talents-common.md) -- they are NOT archetype talents, which is why they don't appear in `talents-t1/t2/dlc.md`. If a talent on your common-talent screen isn't in those archetype files, it is almost certainly a homeworld or origin talent -- look here first.

## Death World
**Stats:** +5 STR, +5 AGI, +5 TGH, -5 FEL, -5 INT · **Feature:** Survival Instinct

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Brutal Hunter | +15% crit chance vs. targets that are bleeding or at <=50% max wounds. |
| Wounded Beast | Every injury gives +5 AGI and +5 WP. Every trauma counts as 3 injuries. |
| Tenacity | Once per combat, when stunned/blinded/immobilised, ignore that condition and instead gain +20% temporary wounds. |
| Trusty Weapons | +10% crit chance with axes, hammers, and las weapons. |
| Hellish Life | Take (TGH bonus) less damage from flame, toxic, and bleeding effects. |

## Forge World
**Stats:** +5 INT, +5 TGH, -5 FEL · **Feature:** Forged for a Purpose (pick a sub-system: Subskin Armour +[5+2x TGH bonus]% armour · Locomotion System +[AGI bonus/2] MP and +[2x AGI bonus]% dodge · Analytic System +[10+INT bonus]% crit chance)

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Pinnacle of Weaponry | First attack in combat with a plasma/melta/power weapon deals +INT bonus damage; for the rest of combat the bonus is +(INT bonus/2). |
| Steel of the Forge | While wearing heavy armour: +1 MP, +1 deflection, cannot fall prone. |
| Calculated Relations | Use Persuasion, Coercion, and Commerce off Intelligence instead of Fellowship. |
| Fires of the Forge | +(TGH bonus) deflection vs. the burning effect and flame weapons. |
| Persistence of the Forge | Stacking +10% hit chance and dodge reduction vs. any target you hit with a single-target attack; single-target crits raise it by +20%. |

## Fortress World
**Stats:** +5 PER, +5 WP, -5 FEL · **Feature:** Never Stop Shooting

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Combat Addict | While in combat: +7 BS/PER/WP. While out of combat: -7 INT/FEL/PER/WP. |
| Familiar Kickback | At end of turn, gain Never Stop Shooting stacks = half the minimum damage of the most damaging ranged weapon used this round. |
| Never Stop Believing | When you would lose all Never Stop Shooting stacks, keep (WP/2) of them instead. Also lets you upgrade WP even if your archetype disallows it. |
| Hail of Steel | Each round, stacking +1% dodge vs. ranged per shot you made this round. Burst attacks also grant +10 Never Stop Shooting stacks. |
| Spare Magazine | First reload in combat costs 0 AP. Reloading grants +10 Never Stop Shooting stacks. |

## Hive World
**Stats:** +5 FEL, +5 AGI, -5 WP · **Feature:** Strength in Numbers

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Comradery | Pass Willpower resistance tests using Fellowship instead, if FEL is higher. |
| Weapon Personalization | All ranged weapons dealing physical damage: +1 damage, +1% armour pen, +1 rate of fire, -5 recoil. |
| Fresh Start | +50% dodge vs. the first attack of opportunity each round. |
| Outnumber | Your melee superiority bonus is increased by +10; enemy melee superiority against you is decreased by -10. (Melee Superiority: a target surrounded by more allies than enemies gives attackers +10 WS per extra ally.) |
| Helping Hand | Start your turn adjacent to an ally -> +2 MP. End your turn adjacent to allies -> they all gain +2 MP next turn. |

## Imperial World
**Stats:** none (the Humanity's Finest feature instead raises any one characteristic by +10) · **Feature:** Humanity's Finest

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Ready to Serve | When you affect an ally (or are affected) by a non-damaging ability, +(FEL bonus/2) resolve for 1 round. |
| Doing My Part | If no other party member is from an Imperial world, the first use of any of your archetype abilities costs -1 AP. |
| Stronger Together | All non-xenos allies gain +5 to the same characteristic you chose for Humanity's Finest. |
| Better to Die for the Emperor | While under 40% wounds: +10 to all characteristics and +2 resolve. |
| Know no Heresy | +10% crit chance and +10% armour vs. xenos/daemonic; allies get half. But your Lore (Xenos) and Lore (Warp) are always 0. |

## Voidborn
**Stats:** +5 WP, +5 INT, -5 STR · **Feature:** Fortune

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bloody Mess | On a critical hit, 10% chance to double the damage. |
| Just a Flesh Wound | 20% chance to survive lethal damage, surviving at 1 wound. |
| Be Smart | Any ability/talent using FEL bonus may use INT bonus instead if higher. Also lets you upgrade INT even if your archetype disallows it. |
| Contagious Luck | Non-damaging ability on an ally: that ally may reroll any failed attack/dodge/parry/characteristic/skill test (20% success chance, capped at the roll's base chance) for 1 round. On an enemy: 20% chance to fail a successful dodge/parry for 1 round. |
| Jinx | While the voidborn character is above 50% wounds, ALL hit chances of ALL creatures (including enemies) within 3 cells are +10%. Below 50% wounds, -10%. (Double-edged luck aura -- it buffs enemies too while you're healthy.) |

> **Reading the common-talent screen:** your picks at the common-talent ranks come from THREE pools combined -- this homeworld set, your [origin set](talents-origin.md), and the [universal pool](talents-common.md) (proficiencies, characteristic training, skill talents, Nimble). The game does not label which pool a talent came from; this file is the homeworld half of that lookup.
