## Arbitrator Origin Sub-Paths (Lex Imperialis DLC)

Arbitrator is an **origin**, not an archetype. Choose one of three sub-paths at character creation. All Arbitrators receive +10 Coercion.

_source: doctor research 2026-05-27 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: dlc:lex_imperialis_

### Common Arbitrator Talents (all sub-paths)

| Talent | Effect |
|---|---|
| Arbites Physical Training | +TGH equal to half equipped shield's block |
| Armour-Piercing | Attacks reduce enemy armour by (10+2x PER bonus) for 1 round (no stack) |
| Binding Purpose | Above 90% wounds -- immune to blinded/disturbed/fatigued/perplexed/slowed/staggered |

### Vigilant (Soldier-leaning)
+5 BS, +5 PER; shotgun mid-range specialisation. Unique talents: **Ruthless Suppression** + **Death Sentence** ability.

### Castigator (Officer-leaning)
+5 WP, +5 FEL. Unique abilities: **That's an Order!** (WP test -- forced move to designated cell), **In the Name of the Lex Imperialis!** (party CC immunity 1 round; enemy WP test or immobilise). Unique talent: **Law and Order** (attacks on others' turns +(10+WP)% damage + WP test or immobilise).

### Subductor (Warrior-leaning)
+5 WS, +5 TGH. Unique ability: **Assault Onslaught** (5-cell dash through allies/enemies, AGI test or prone, requires shield). Shared with Vigilant: **Ruthless Suppression**. Shield-block bonuses.

## Overseer Familiar Talent Branches (Lex Imperialis DLC)

Choose one Familiar type when taking Overseer. Each has a distinct talent branch. Overseer shared talents in [`talents-t2.md`](talents-t2.md).

_source: doctor research 2026-05-27 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: dlc:lex_imperialis_

**Note:** Familiar talents split across multiple Fextralife sub-pages; some text sourced from Google snippets. Confidence medium.

### Cyber-Mastiff Proficiency
Best for: Subductor/Warrior/Soldier MC.

Core auto-abilities: Apprehend!, Battle Mode: Standby, Protect!, Flanking Manoeuvre.

Key talents include archetype-gated branches:
- Warrior: Combat Reflexes share with Mastiff
- Officer: VoC propagation to Mastiff
- Operative: exploits maintained on Mastiff hits
- Soldier: reduces ally reload AP
- Bladedancer: call-of-blood per missing wounds

Notable talents:
- **Stalwart Mastiff:** temp wounds -- +(10+temp wounds)% hit/damage/armour/dodge/parry
- **"Heel!" Protocol:** +25 WS/STR, +400% damage, Heel! ability
- Apprehend synergy and pursuit talent chain

### Psyber-Raven Proficiency
Best for: Psyker MC (MC-recommended; relay mechanic effectively doubles ability output).

Core auto-abilities: Purification Discharge, Relocate.

Key talents:
- **Psy Relay:** non-attacking single-target psychic powers extrapolated 4-cell radius around raven
- **Purification Discharge Mastery:** connected targets share mental damage
- **Mental Suppression:** enemies near raven -(2x WP bonus) WP
- **Advanced Tracking:** relayed power -- random enemy immobilised
- **Blessing of the Immaterium:** raven +30 WP; relayed attacks as if +1 Psy Rating
- **Warp Movement / Active Relay / Warp Charge:** movement damage, +1 AP per relay/round, +3 WP per relay
- **Overcharge** (Heroic/Desperate variants): raven full heal, next 3 abilities -1 AP, can relay attacking psychic powers end of combat

### Cyber-Eagle Proficiency
Best for: Operative/Soldier MC (exploit synergy stacks with Operative MC).

Core auto-ability: Obstruct Vision.

Key talents:
- **Exploit Synergy:** eagle gets your damage bonus vs. exploits, removes exploits, obstruct -- AGI test or +exploits
- **Block Ignore**
- **Soaring Stance:** 10% max wounds regen per round while soaring
- **Air Support:** +10 AGI/PER, +150% damage, +crit on obstructed
- **Blood Feast:** +20 PER, +300% damage, +25% crit vs. bleeding, auto-attack obstructed enemy chains
- **Last Stand:** lethal damage -- soar + 33% max wounds
- Soaring Ascent / Deflective Glide / Tactical Takeoff / Aerial Refinement

### Servo-Skull Swarm Proficiency
Best for: Officer MC (extrapolation turns single-target Officer buffs into AoE).

Core auto-abilities: Extrapolation, Relocation.

Key talents:
- **Swarm Extrapolation:** non-attacking single-target abilities propagate to 4-cell radius
- **Cover Boost:** partial cover treated as full
- **Analytics Module:** extrapolation -- stacking +5 INT/FEL
- **Knowledge is Power:** +1 FEL on allies / +1 INT on enemies (stacks)
- **Tactical Distribution:** first extrapolation per round +1 AP
- **Lifelink:** ally lethal damage -- ally gets (FEL bonus/3)% max wounds; swarm shock damage
- **Long-Range Coordination:** 5+ cells from swarm -- +(2x FEL bonus) FEL
- **Bring-It-Down Loop:** chain extra turn -- swarm +5 all characteristics
- **Extra-Turn Bonus:** any extra turn in 4-cell radius of swarm -- +3 FEL

**Servo-Skull Swarm community note:** Extrapolation with Voice of Command community-confirmed working but not documented in tooltips (medium confidence).
