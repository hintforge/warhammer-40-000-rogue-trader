## Weapon Families and Proficiency

Each family requires a single 1-point Proficiency talent. No multi-step "feat tax" class -- all proficiencies are single 1-point investments. Exception noted below.

### Melee families (6)

| Family | Proficiency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive | none / minimal | Basic blunt and edged |
| Chain | Chain Weapon Proficiency | Chainswords, chainaxes |
| Power | Power Weapon Proficiency | Power swords, axes, mauls; strong vs armor |
| Force | Force Weapon Proficiency | Psyker-focused; bonus damage from Psy Rating |
| Blade | Blade Proficiency | Fast, mobile; pairs with Assassin/Bladedancer |
| Rocksaw | Rocksaw Proficiency | Heavy melee; industrial |

### Ranged families (7)

| Family | Proficiency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Solid Projectile | Solid Projectile Proficiency | Heavy stubbers, autoguns; calibrated heavy stubber is dominant Ch2+ ranged build (1 AP for 12-round burst) |
| Las | Las Weapon Proficiency | Standard reliable; moderate damage |
| Bolter | Bolt Weapon Proficiency | **Heavy bolters do NOT require Bolt Weapon Proficiency** (patch-current quirk; Chris Williams GameFAQs) |
| Plasma | Plasma Proficiency | High damage; heat risk |
| Flamer | Flamer Proficiency | AoE fire damage; good vs Chaos Cultists, Genestealers |
| Melta | Melta Proficiency | Anti-armor point-blank |
| Exotic / Drukhari | Drukhari Weapon Proficiency | Faction weapons; high dodge synergy; Heavy Weapon Proficiency reduces STR requirement by 25 |

### Power Armour note (near-feat-tax)

Power Armour Proficiency is effectively a feat tax for most builds: only 3 suits exist in the base game, and none arrive before late Ch4 / ~level 45. Patch 1.5 / Lex Imperialis adds 3 more Power Armour variants. Still not recommended to invest the point before Ch3 unless targeting an armored build.

## Shields

Shields are a distinct equipment slot added by the Lex Imperialis DLC. Equipped in the off-hand; provide passive armor/dodge bonuses and in some cases active block abilities. Base-game has no shield class. DLC Foehammer weapon and Familiar Protocols interact with Arbitrator shield builds.

_source: P3 research cascade 2026-05-23 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: dlc:Lex Imperialis_

## Patch 1.5 Balance Changes (Void Shadows + Lex Imperialis patch)

- **Plasma weapons:** Damage and range substantially buffed; elevated to S-tier in community tier lists post-patch.
- **Shotguns:** Pattern and spread expanded; improved vs. grouped enemies.
- **Webber / Arc rifle:** Crowd-control reliability improved; better stun consistency.
- **Shotgun expansion** (Lex Imperialis DLC only): Enforcer Shotgun variant available to Arbitrator builds.
