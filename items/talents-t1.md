## Officer (T1)
_stat focus: FEL, WP, then TGH/BS_

**Patch notes:** Patch 1.1.28 (Feb 19 2024): Bring It Down! can no longer be applied to the same ally more than once per round; Officers no longer generate Momentum multiple times a turn by giving bonus turns to allies. Patch 1.3: Finest Hour! Upgrade IV redesigned to -1 AP on non-attack abilities.

| Talent | Effect | Recommended? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stronghold (T1) | Ally targeted by temp-wound ability also gets +10% armour 1 round (no stack) | Yes | Cassia/Vanguard synergy |
| Be Vigilant! | Allies under your abilities get +(3+FEL bonus) WS and +(FEL bonus) AoO damage | Yes (melee party) | Mutually exclusive with Focus! in practice |
| Focus! | Ability on ally grants +(2+Resolve/2) PER and BS | Yes (ranged party) | Mutually exclusive with Be Vigilant! in practice |
| Inspiring Resolve | Heroic Act by ally generates temp wounds = (10% max + your resolve) | Yes | Mandatory with high resolve |
| Commanding Voice | Voice of Command range +6; other ability range +3 | Yes | Mandatory for positioning |
| Personal Oversight | VoC and Bring It Down! cost -1 AP on first use | Yes | Top action economy |
| Lasting Impression | Half of VoC's bonus persists end of combat | Yes | One of the strongest Officer talents |
| Solitary Authority | Two non-extra-turn Officer abilities in a round -- next one also affects you | Yes | Self-buff loop |
| Steel Resolve | Every attack you make grants +5 FEL end of combat | Situational | Only if you actually attack |
| Unflinching Heroism | AP-granting abilities also give +1 to all characteristics per AP (end of combat) | Yes | Insane scaling -- top pick |
| Logistical Superiority | MP-granting ability also gives you half that many MP | Yes | Free reposition |
| No Respite | Stacking characteristic bonuses on AP grant (pairs with Unflinching Heroism) | Yes | Bring It Down! combo |
| More Than Possible | Bring It Down! target gains +1 extra AP (suffers WP damage if unspent) | Yes | Signature Officer combo; nerfed in 1.1.28 (once/ally/round) |
