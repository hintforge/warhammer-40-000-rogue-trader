**status:** research-integrated

## System Rules

- **4 consumable slots per character.** Items do not stack within slots.
- Most consume 1-2 AP and a Combat Action.
- No ammunition management -- ranged weapons reload between encounters at no cost; ammo is not a managed resource.
- No crafting system in base game.
