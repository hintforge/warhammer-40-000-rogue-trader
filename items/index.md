**"Available Common Talents" screen = THREE pools combined.** When the game offers a Common Talent at level-up, the options are drawn from your **homeworld** ([`talents-homeworld.md`](talents-homeworld.md)) + **origin** ([`talents-origin.md`](talents-origin.md)) + the **universal pool** ([`talents-common.md`](talents-common.md)) -- NOT the archetype trees in `talents-t1/t2/psyker/dlc.md`. If a player names a talent off that screen and it isn't an archetype talent, check those three files in order.

**Confirmed absent (do not create stubs):**
- Crafting materials -- no crafting system in base game; items are looted / traded / quest-rewarded
- Ammo/cartridges -- no ammunition management; ranged weapons reload between encounters at no cost
- Support/deployables -- no discrete deployable category; only psyker summons and companion familiars (see `abilities.md` Support Items section)

**DLC items (Void Shadows, Lex Imperialis):** deferred to P3.
