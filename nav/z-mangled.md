## Entry

## Entry tips

1. **Ugly Beggar** -- Kill for stimms + boots loot. Gives Pasqal's location clue. Approach before sweeping the area.
2. **Abelard rescue** -- Athletics jump required to reach him; prioritize Athletics-capable party member for this.
3. **Cthonos optional boss** -- Athletics check to access the arena area. Fight from the edge (sniper tactic) to avoid melee range.
