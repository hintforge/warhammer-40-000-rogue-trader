## Entry

E08 from z-chartist (one-way, permanent). Player wakes with no gear.

## Entry tips

1. You arrive with **no equipment** -- don't panic; a starter kit is available via Medicae check on the gladiator, and additional weapons are in the gladiators' pile.
2. Check the **Awareness pillar** for a hidden compartment with extra gear before speaking to Malice.
3. Malice's errand (kill the Commissar) leads to the Streets of the Chasm; do not try to exit before completing it.

## Sequential gates

1. **Wake on platform** -- No gear. Zone entry from E08.
   - `point_of_no_return:` n/a (already committed)
2. **Medicae check on gladiator** -- Receive starter kit.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Gladiators' pile** -- Additional mediocre weapons.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
4. **Awareness pillar switch** -- Hidden compartment; extra gear.
   - `lock:` Awareness check
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
5. **Malice dialogue** -- Errand to kill Commissar (sends player to Streets of the Chasm).
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
6. **Exit to Streets of the Chasm** (z-chasm) -- Via portal behind Commissar location.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none

## Common confusions

- "I have no weapons" -- Starter kit via Medicae check on the nearby gladiator; pile also has weapons.
- The Awareness pillar is easy to walk past; check the visible pillar structures before proceeding.
