## Entry

From z-foot-dock (east gate). Sub-zone of Footfall hub.

## Entry tips

1. The **Pasqal upper-cogitator** (northeast of bar area) requires Pasqal to operate -- it opens the gate to a chest (Unyielding Vanguard Boots). Easy to miss.
2. **Jae's introduction bar fight** (Persona Non Grata) is triggered here; completing it opens the sewer route via z-foot-shadow. Missing both Jae AND the Fidelio funeral chain permanently locks Rat Hunting.
3. **Chapel of Saint Drusus** in the SW held one of the four Dreams and Stories hidden caches (Ch.2 only window).

## Optional branches

- **Underworld Fidelio chain** -- Fidelio impersonation requires NOT announcing as Rogue Trader at z-foot-dock; gates the Crematorium-to-sewers route via z-foot-shadow as an alternative to Jae.
- **Daggen (SW bait point)** -- Fidelio-related NPC; approach for quest trigger.
- **Dreams and Stories caches** -- Chapel of Saint Drusus is one of four Ch.2-only cache locations. Required for achievement.

## Common confusions

- Missing Pasqal's cogitator (northeast bar area) is extremely common -- it's not on the main route.
- Both Jae and Fidelio chains are sewer-access routes; missing BOTH is the soft-lock, not just missing one.

## Soft-lock warnings

- **Rat Hunting permanently locked** -- Both sewer access routes close if: (a) Jae not recruited via Persona Non Grata AND (b) Fidelio/Underworld funeral route not taken. Complete at least one.

## Exit

To z-foot-liege (north), z-foot-dock (south/west), or z-foot-shadow (via quest-gated routes).
