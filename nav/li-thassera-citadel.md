## Entry tips

1. "One Step Ahead" achievement: collect every conspiracy clue before the final Solomorne briefing. Clue collection must be done before leaving Thassera permanently.
2. "Royal Feast": all three dish checks at the banquet must pass in one visit.
3. Patch 1.5 added extensive NPC banter and animation to this zone; earlier patches had Solomorne stuck at overleveled recruitment.

## Sequential gates

1. **Citadel dinner (banquet hall)** -- "Royal Feast" achievement: pass all three dish checks. One-scene window; cannot return after leaving scene.
2. **Conspiracy clue collection** -- "One Step Ahead" achievement: every clue required before final briefing.
   - `point_of_no_return:` PoNR-LI-02: leaving Thassera after final-stage dialogue locks Sister and Governor fates.
3. **Final Solomorne briefing** -- triggers PoNR-LI-02.
