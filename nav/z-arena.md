## Entry

From z-chasm (NW arena portal, after first-arena unlock).

## Entry tips

1. The **first arena** has captive human soldiers as opponents -- if all humans die (rather than the Khymerae), the "Those Who Are About to Die" achievement fires.
2. **Save before the Sergeant Vigastes encounter** -- Iconoclast + ranged approach is the condition for a good outcome.
3. **Heretical Dust of Rending** -- Available from the Pit ritual in a specific branch; only one path leads to it.

## Sequential gates

## Optional branches

- **Heretical Dust of Rending** -- Via Pit ritual branch; Heretical-only reward.

## Common confusions

- E09 (exit to webway) fires automatically after the final fight. Ensure all Commorragh exploration (Mangled, Opera, Ulfar, gear) is complete before the final arena fight.

## Soft-lock warnings

None beyond E09 (which locks all Ch.3 side content -- ensure sweep is complete before entering arena).

## Exit

E09 -> z-webway (Spire of the Reaving Tempest). One-way, chapter-bound.
