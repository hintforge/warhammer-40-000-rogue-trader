## Entry

li-neos-charoitus-system <-> li-leethus-surface (two-way).

## Entry tips

1. "Of Course I Know What I'm Doing!" (hidden): Lord Captain must personally drive the tank -- triggers on entering the tank yourself (not letting a companion do it).
2. "Tank You!": set up 7+ enemies in tank shot range before firing.
3. "Rogue Trader, One Item": allow Tertius Quart (inventory clerk) to fully document your inventory. Do not skip his dialogue.
4. "Thorough Audit": open every container on Leethus -- includes sand-zone chest requiring Glaito.

## Sequential gates

1. **Tank acquisition** -- opens the tank scene. RT must personally enter tank for "Of Course I Know What I'm Doing!".
2. **Watcher tower confrontation** -- "Family Secret" (hidden): learn the Watcher's identity and keep the tithe for yourself. Requires Commerce check.
3. **Inventory clerk (Tertius Quart)** -- "Rogue Trader, One Item": let him fully document RT inventory. Single dialogue scene.

## Exit

Two-way back to li-neos-charoitus-system; two-way to li-leethus-sand-zone.

## Landmarks

Tertius Quart's clerk desk; Leethus tank parking; Watcher's tower; sand dune ridge.
