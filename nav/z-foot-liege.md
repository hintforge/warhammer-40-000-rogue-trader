## Entry

From z-foot-atrium (north exit). Vladaym Tocara's audience hall.

## Entry tips

1. Tocara's audience triggers **Jae's expulsion** from Footfall; complete Persona Non Grata in z-foot-atrium before this scene if you want Jae's chain active.
2. Faction traders unlock after the **Hunger's Hand quest** starts here -- check their inventory before Ch.3.
3. In Ch.4: **Vladaym/Ryzza eavesdrop** is only available at z-foot-martyr, not here -- go there after revisiting Liege.

## Sequential gates

1. **Vladaym Tocara audience hall** -- Expels Jae; Hunger's Hand quest start.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none (but triggers Jae expulsion; have Persona Non Grata started first)
2. **Hunger's Hand quest start** -- Triggers faction-trader unlock in this zone.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Faction traders** -- Available after Hunger's Hand triggers. Buy before Ch.3.
   - `point_of_no_return:` chapter-bound (Footfall cordoned Ch.3)
4. **Exit back to Atrium** -- Standard return path.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none

## Optional branches

- **Octaviana** -- Rumors NPC at desk; provides zone/story context.
- **Ryzza side-room** -- Kasballica trader available here and at the Atrium bar.

## Common confusions

- Faction traders only appear after Hunger's Hand is started in this zone -- visit Tocara first.

## Soft-lock warnings

- **Ch.4 Heretical path divergence (E11)** -- Refusing to repent at Liege audience in Ch.4 (Heretical track) triggers a point-of-divergence with potential companion loyalty breaks. Manual save before Ch.4 Liege visit.

## Exit

To z-foot-atrium (south return).
