## Entry

From z-pit (forward progression through Malice's errand).

## Entry tips

1. The **Anatomical Opera (northeast)** is where Tervantias is -- go there before the second arena for gear retrieval and Ulfar's recruitment (Bone Key).
2. **Yrliet rescue at the crossroads** -- Kill or recruit her. Recruiting here is required for her to remain in the party (attacking her = permanent removal).
3. After the first arena, **Mangled Sector (lift, southeast)** contains Abelard and Pasqal rescue targets.

## Sequential gates

1. **Yrliet rescue at crossroads** -- Kill or recruit Yrliet. Recruit to retain her as companion.
   - `point_of_no_return:` permanent (killing/letting die removes Yrliet permanently)

   > see crew/yrliet-lanaevyss.md for full companion summary
2. **Mangled Sector lift (SE)** -- Leads to z-mangled; Abelard and Pasqal companions are there.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **First arena (forced)** -- Either Malice or Commissar side. Forced combat.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
4. **Gear retrieval at Tervantias + Mon-Keigh Rubbish box** -- NE Opera entrance. Party gear returned after first arena win. Requires Tervantias dialogue.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
5. **Cthonos optional miniboss (Mangled, post-first-arena)** -- Athletics-gated in z-mangled. Optional.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
6. **Second arena (Marazhai)** -- Marazhai fight. Proceed here after Mangled and Opera sweeps.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
7. **Tervantias evacuation cue** -- After second arena; move to Spire of the Reaving Tempest (z-arena).
   - `point_of_no_return:` chapter-bound (triggers forward progression to E09)

## Optional branches

- **Mangled Sector (z-mangled)** -- Abelard/Pasqal rescue + Cthonos optional boss. Must do before second arena.
- **Opera (z-opera)** -- Tervantias gear retrieval + psyker recruitment + Ulfar (Bone Key). Must visit before second arena; missing Ulfar's Bone Key here locks him out permanently (patch 1.5+).

## Common confusions

- "I can't find Ulfar" -- He's in z-opera (NE), not in the main Chasm streets. Go to the Opera before the second arena.
- "Picking 'I guess I have to kill you' with Argenta during the Commissar fight makes her hostile" -- This choice likely wipes the team. Manual save before the Commissar encounter.

## Soft-lock warnings

- **Argenta hostile trigger** -- "I guess I have to kill you" option during Commissar fight can make her hostile and cause a team wipe (Neoseeker Reunion).

## Exit

To z-mangled (SE lift), z-opera (NE), z-arena (NW arena portal). z-pit (south, rear exit only -- forward progression continues to arena).
