**Type:** hub-entry
**Linear?:** no (hub zone)
**Chapter:** 2 & 4 (cordoned Ch.3; partially cordoned post-Dargonus inauguration)

## Entry

Shuttle from Furibundus system map. All Footfall sub-zones are reached through this entry point.

## Entry tips

1. The **SW four-button crate** uses sequence 3-5-4-1 (not 1-2-3-4). Reward: Interrogation Goggles + Psyker's Footwear.
2. **Do NOT announce as Rogue Trader** at the dock if you plan to do the Underworld Fidelio impersonation quest chain -- that announcement blocks it permanently (Neoseeker Tattered_Spirit).
3. **Footfall traders close at Dargonus inauguration** -- buy everything you need before the Ch.2->Ch.3 transition. No access until Ch.4.

## Sequential gates

1. **Bloodstained Amelia ambush** -- Amelia + 12 Anver Thugs + 4 Armoured Anvers. Forced combat on arrival.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
2. **SW four-button crate puzzle** -- Code: 3-5-4-1. Reward: Interrogation Goggles + Psyker's Footwear.
   - `lock:` sequence 3-5-4-1 -- see architecture.md locks-and-keys
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Opticon-22** -- Explorator trader (south). Explorator rep purchases available.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none (trader accessible while Footfall is open)
4. **East exit to Atrium** -- Leads to z-foot-atrium.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
5. **South exit to Shadow Quarters** -- Leads to z-foot-shadow.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none

## Optional branches

- **Trade with Opticon-22** -- Explorator reputation items; accessible throughout Ch.2.

## Common confusions

- "Underworld Fidelio chain is unavailable" -- announcing as Rogue Trader at the dock blocks this quest permanently. Arrive incognito to keep the chain open.

## Soft-lock warnings

None confirmed (but see chapter-cordon warning: all Footfall zones are effectively inaccessible Ch.3 except for partial Ch.4 return).

## Exit

To z-foot-atrium (east), z-foot-shadow (south), or shuttle to system map.
