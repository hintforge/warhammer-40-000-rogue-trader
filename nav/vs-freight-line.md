## Entry

z-ship-lower -> vs-freight-line (two-way, open from Chapter 1 once DLC active).

## Entry tips

1. **"It Itches!" achievement setup:** do NOT talk to the ship's doctor about the rash if going for this achievement. Catch the rash and never cure it through end of game.
2. **"At Home Among the Poor" achievement:** mingle with every lower-deck NPC type here before the Freight Line collapse event.
3. "Trolley Problem" achievement requires Jinevra alive from Act 2.

## Sequential gates

None mandatory -- this zone is fully physical exploration.

## Optional branches

- **Trolley quest (saves Captain Klein):** requires Jinevra alive from Act 2; "Trolley Problem" achievement.
- **Rash debuff window:** interact with infected area; triggers "It Itches!" achievement run.
- **Crane container drop:** "Mind Your Head" achievement -- drop container on yellow-zone worker.
- **"At Home Among the Poor":** talk to all lower-deck NPC types before FL collapse cutscene.

## Common confusions

- Leaving FL while the collapse cutscene is staged can skip "At Home Among the Poor"; complete all NPC interactions first.

## Soft-lock warnings

- "Trolley Problem" permanently locked if Jinevra died in Act 2.
- "It Itches!" locked if rash is cured at any point before game end.

## Exit

Back to z-ship-lower; lateral to vs-overlook-walkway.
