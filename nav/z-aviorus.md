## Entry

Scan Aviorus system on galactic chart. Recommended entry: character level 20 (TheGamer guide).

## Entry tips

1. **Do not bring Idira or Heinrix as primary party members** -- the zone has high Veil Degradation at start, which hurts psyker-heavy parties (TheGamer guide).
2. The **backup-generator room** puzzle (5 stabilizers, pressure to 10) gates the Compressor Hall main cogitator. See corpus puzzles/ for stabilizer positions (2 near panel + far one bottom-right).
3. **Save before Magos Tiberius Mahla dialogue** -- choosing to kill for Daemonic Presence voidship ability vs. fighting the Forgefiend is irreversible.

## Sequential gates

1. **Entrance corridor** -- Locked main door, Tech-Use 0 check opens it.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
2. **Sliding-door corridor** -- Navigation; approach to turret puzzle.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Electric-turret puzzle** -- Send one party member through corridor; pull lever to disable turrets for others.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
4. **Augmented Gloves chest** -- Loot in side area post-turrets.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
5. **Backup-generator room** -- 5 stabilizers puzzle; pressure to 10. See corpus puzzles/. Gates access to sealed door.
   - `lock:` puzzle (5 stabilizers) -- see architecture.md locks-and-keys
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
6. **Toxic-gas chamber** -- Navigate through or resolve.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
7. **Compressor Hall main cogitator** -- Interactable after generator puzzle; opens sealed door.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
8. **Sealed door opens** -- Path to Forgefiend arena.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
9. **Forgefiend boss arena** -- Combat encounter.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
   _source: P2 research cascade 2026-05-23 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
10. **Magos Tiberius Mahla dialogue** -- Kill Mahla for loot OR fight Forgefiend to take it for voidship Daemonic Presence ability. Irreversible choice.
    - `point_of_no_return:` permanent

## Optional branches

- **Breakable wall in entrance corridor** -- Melta Charge breaks it; Tech-Use check on safe behind. Loot reward.

## Common confusions

- "High Veil Degradation at zone start seems bugged" -- This is an intended zone property; swap Idira/Heinrix out of primary party before entering.
- Generator puzzle: 2 stabilizers near control panel + 1 far one at bottom-right. Total pressure target: 10.
