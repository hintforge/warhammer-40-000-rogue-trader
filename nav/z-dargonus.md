## Entry

Shuttle from Mundus Valancius system.

## Entry tips

1. The **bedchamber saferoom** is hidden -- interact with the book on the nightstand to reveal the couch; then complete the couch sequence (Flame of Purity wine + Litanies of the Motive Force book + Symphony of Honour Pt 2 music). Required for "How Did It All End?" chain.
2. The **Dargonus inauguration ceremony (Ch.2)** is the hard PoNR for Ch.2->Ch.3: after it fires, Footfall traders are cordoned until Ch.4 and Shadow Quarters is gone.
3. **Complete Foulstone (Trinnitos)** before inaugurating -- Dargonus tier-5 colony + Three Sorrowful Phalanges chain depend on it.

## Sequential gates

1. **Noble greeting at shuttle pad** -- Introductory Clementia/Achilleas NPC scene.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
2. **Throne room dialogue** -- Clementia/Achilleas discussion; leads to Governor meeting.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Governor meeting** -- Vheabos VI rumor surface.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
4. **Noble-house pick for inauguration ceremony** -- Choose which noble house to favor. Downstream reputation effects.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none (selection, not irreversible until ceremony fires)
5. **Bedchamber saferoom** (optional, Ch.2 window) -- Book on nightstand -> couch -> Flame of Purity wine + Litanies book + Symphony Pt 2. See architecture.md locks-and-keys.
   - `point_of_no_return:` missable-trigger (access to saferoom closes after inauguration)
6. **Inauguration ceremony cinematic** -- Aurora apparition fires. **HARD Ch.2->Ch.3 PoNR.** Footfall traders cordoned; Shadow Quarters inaccessible.
   - `point_of_no_return:` permanent (Steam 4038104984934310792 user Morgian: "Chapter 2 ends with your inthronisation on Dargonus -- you can no longer access the traders then until chapter 4")
7. **Drukhari assault (Blades in the Void)** -- Triggered by inauguration; combat phase.
   - `point_of_no_return:` chapter-bound

## Optional branches

- **Gardens (Abelard's family quest)** -- Ch.4 window; Abelard's companion quest resolution.
- **Colony tier progression** -- Completing tier-5 colony project triggers the 6th Dargonus colony event which unlocks the Three Sorrowful Phalanges system (Fextralife).

## Common confusions

- "All Footfall traders are gone" -- Dargonus inauguration cordoned them. Ch.4 return restores partial access (under inquisitorial martial law).
- Bedchamber saferoom input order matters -- wrong inputs trigger a security cutscene. Input: nightstand book first, then couch interactions in correct sequence.

## Soft-lock warnings

- **Inauguration PoNR** -- The single most important pre-Ch.3 checkpoint. Buy all Footfall merchant goods, complete Shadow Quarters content, and finish optional Ch.2 quests before the ceremony.

## Exit

Shuttle to Mundus Valancius system. Inauguration triggers E07 (one-way Ch.2->Ch.3).
