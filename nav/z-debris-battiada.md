## Entry

Warp from Ch.4 end into Ch.5 systems.

## Entry tips

1. Three skill tests at the signal source yield the **Polyglot achievement** -- attempt all three consecutively.
2. The **main cogitator (Tech-Use -10)** is the progress gate; easy check at Ch.5 level.
3. When the alarm triggers after extracting data, the Necron wave is large but manageable -- have the party in defensive positions before activating the console.
