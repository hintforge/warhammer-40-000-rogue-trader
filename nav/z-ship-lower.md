## Entry

Via section-map from any voidship zone. Expanded access unlocks in Ch.2 via Dark Echelons quest.

## Entry tips

1. The **valve puzzle** corridor (5 valves near the goods crate) yields loot -- solve it before triggering the Macro-Cannon Chamber quest sequence.
2. **Idira must be excluded** from the party when triggering Echo of the Past (the Macro-Cannon Chamber event); her presence prevents the quest from firing.
3. Save manually before **Idira's dialogue choice** at the end of Echo of the Past -- it has 5 outcomes including her permanent death.

## Sequential gates

1. **Lower Decks valve puzzle** -- 5 valves in sequence → goods crate reward. Optional; see corpus puzzles/ if stuck.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
2. **Party composition check** -- Echo of the Past requires Idira NOT in active party. Reassign before entering Macro-Cannon Chamber.
   - `point_of_no_return:` missable-trigger (wrong party composition may prevent quest trigger; Fextralife user reports)
3. **Macro-Cannon Chamber approach** -- Via valve corridor. Echo of the Past quest fires here.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none (quest trigger)
4. **Daemonic apparition encounter** -- Boss fight: Theodora-as-Pink Horror.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
   _source: P2 research cascade 2026-05-23 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
5. **Idira dialogue choice** -- Major choice post-fight; 5 outcomes. Permanent consequence possible.
   - `point_of_no_return:` permanent (some outcomes are irreversible)
   _source: P2 research cascade 2026-05-23 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_

## Optional branches

- **Dark Echelons quest** -- Expands lower deck access in Ch.2.

## Common confusions

- "Echo of the Past won't trigger" -- Idira must NOT be in the active party when you approach (Fextralife community reports).
- The valve puzzle and the Macro-Cannon Chamber are in the same corridor; complete the puzzle before the quest fires.

## Soft-lock warnings

None confirmed, but the Idira outcome at step 5 is permanent. Manual save strongly recommended before that dialogue.

## Exit

Via section-map to z-ship-bridge / z-ship-upper / z-ship-cargo / z-ship-quarters (always).
