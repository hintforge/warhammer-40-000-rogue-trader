## Entry

li-mundus-nullius-system -> li-heartless-bridge (one-way boarding; E-LI-01).

## Entry tips

1. "Not a Scratch" requires no compartment destruction during the void-battle leadup -- decide before boarding.
2. Optional pirate persuasion before the bridge fight can reduce combat difficulty.

## Sequential gates

1. **Optional turret destruction** -- "Not a Scratch" locked if any turrets are destroyed during boarding approach.
2. **Optional pirate persuasion** -- negotiation dialogue reduces crew hostility.
3. **Captain Sargona fight** -- bridge boss fight; "I'm the Captain Here!" achievement fires on completion.
   - `point_of_no_return:` PoNR-LI-01 after Sargona's defeat -- outcome of captured frigate locked (keep / sell to Arbites / scuttle).

## Locks and keys

- **Bone Key** -- obtained from engine compartment post-fight; opens spherical xeno-prison in li-heartless-engine ("A Slight Sting" achievement).

## Soft-lock warnings

- Pre-1.5: elevator could lock on engine deck; fixed in Patch 1.5.

## Exit

Two-way to li-heartless-engine during boarding. One-way exit E-LI-02: li-heartless-engine -> z-ship-bridge after boarding complete.

## Autosaves

Zone entry; pre-bridge fight.

## Landmarks

Captain's pulpit; Sargona's holo-table; Drukhari shield generator; turret mounts.
