## Entry

Shuttle from Rykad Minoris system map (accessible alongside z-rykad-minoris).

## Entry tips

1. The **Pasqal recruitment trigger** fires at the Reactor antechamber -- speak with him before committing to the reactor core decision, or he may not join.
2. There is a **hidden cache** (Awareness +30 check) off the main path; check side rooms before the reactor core.
3. The **reactor core decision** (purify / preserve / destroy) has downstream consequences; save before it.

## Sequential gates

1. **Monastery entrance** -- Corrupted servitors in entry corridor. Combat introduction to the zone.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
2. **Reactor antechamber** -- Pasqal recruitment trigger. Speak to recruit him as a companion.
   - `point_of_no_return:` missable-trigger (failing recruitment dialogue locks out Pasqal)
   _source: P2 research cascade 2026-05-23 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · entity: pasqal-umberto-i · entity-status: convertible_
   > see crew/pasqal-umberto-i.md for full companion summary
3. **Reactor core** -- Three-way decision: purify / preserve / destroy. Irreversible after confirmation.
   - `point_of_no_return:` permanent (reactor state locked after choice)
4. **Exit shuttle** -- Zone clear; return to system map.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none

## Optional branches

- **Hidden cache** -- Awareness +30 check in side room; loot reward. No routing consequence.

## Common confusions

- Pasqal's recruitment is at the antechamber, before the reactor decision -- players who rush to the core sometimes miss the trigger.

## Soft-lock warnings

None confirmed.

## Exit

Shuttle to Rykad system map.
