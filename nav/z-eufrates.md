## Entry

Shuttle after orbital fleet battle (vs. Blasphemous Styx + book-event assault).

## Entry tips

1. **Equip fire-protection gear** before the west fire-doors -- the Incongruous Defiler beyond has very high HP (~3000) and the fire rooms are hazardous without protection.
2. The **Doomscream branch** (Ulfar's quest resolution) is off the left fork near the main area -- take it before going right (final boss). Easy to miss in linear flow.
3. If the **war-council bridge cutscene** refused to trigger before entry (Ch.4 Janris bug), verify game files (Steam 4030222834120608849) before landing here.
