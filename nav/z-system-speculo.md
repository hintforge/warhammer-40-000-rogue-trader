## Entry

## Entry tips

1. The **energy beam puzzle** is the main progression gate -- the solution pattern is shown on the floor; read the floor before activating.
2. After the puzzle, a second locked-box sequence yields an Aeldari helmet + necklace.
3. The force-sword reward at the Xeno Altar is significant for certain builds.

## Sequential gates

1. **Approach altar** -- Zone entry.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none

   - `lock:` puzzle -- see architecture.md locks-and-keys

   - `point_of_no_return:` none

   - `point_of_no_return:` none
