## Entry

Shuttle from Rykad System map. Landing at the Starport.

## Entry tips

1. The **Warehouse circuit puzzle** (relay-damper-motive force) and the **Upperway orb** are both in this zone -- see corpus puzzles/ for solutions; save before each in case of bugs.
2. Sgt. Malgar assistance early carries to the Ch.1 finale; don't skip his side chain at the Starport.
3. **Malediction of Rykad Minoris** (-5 WP permanent) applies if you linger on Rykad Minoris after Aurora's confrontation. Exit promptly once the Escape quest is complete.

## Optional branches

- **Sgt. Malgar assistance** -- Early help at Starport carries to Ch.1 finale. No extra cost; do it first.
- **Adeptus Mechanicus Monastery side exit** -- Leads to z-electrodyn. Accessible from Rykad Minoris system map; not a detour through the main zone.

## Common confusions

- "Two doors in the warehouse corridor" -- both lead to the same room; choose either without fear of missing content.
- "Upperway orb prompts won't appear" -- reload save and retry; this is a known intermittent bug (nolandchaliha.com).

## Soft-lock warnings

- **Malediction of Rykad Minoris** -- Staying in the zone after Aurora confrontation inflicts a permanent -5 WP debuff. Exit via shuttle as soon as Escape completes.

## Exit

Shuttle to Rykad System map. Chapter 1 finale auto-transition fires once Escape completes (E05 -- one-way chapter-bound exit).
