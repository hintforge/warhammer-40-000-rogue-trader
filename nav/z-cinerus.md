## Entry

System-map navigation within Cinerus Maleficum nebula (Litoribum Maledicta, Lapidus Mortus, Last Chance of Cyrene).

## Entry tips

1. **Last Chance of Cyrene** is the key node -- Mysterious Object scan triggers Blades in the Void sequence and auto-jump to Dargonus at end.
2. **Stash of Children of Asuryan** is also in this cluster -- required for Yrliet's Aeldari in Distress quest completion.
3. The plasma-drive boarding sequence involves a conviction choice; save before the dying-officer scene.
