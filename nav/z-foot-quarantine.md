## Entry tips

1. This zone requires **Kiava Gamma (Reclaim What Was Lost) to be complete** before the gate opens in z-foot-shadow.
2. Complete everything here before leaving -- no known re-entry complication, but the Ch.3 cordon locks Footfall out entirely.
3. Interior map detail is thin in English sources; see corpus quests/Where_the_Shadows for detailed quest guidance.
