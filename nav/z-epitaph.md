## Entry

Shuttle after Nomos-body decision (Asclepius or Forgefiend body). Final dungeon.

## Entry tips

1. **Tech-Use -55 locked crate** near the landing platform (Rack and Ruin foehammers + Astral Currents boots) requires high Tech-Use -- bring it or accept missing that loot.
2. The **Lore: Xenos -90 obelisk** near the end spawns a crate with Singer of Fearsome Sagas + Rallying Boots + Mind Assemble Circlet; very high check.
3. **Save before the C'tan binding console** -- it triggers E14 (final boss, one-way, permanent).

## Optional branches

None -- all content above is linear; loot checks are all optional upgrades.

## Common confusions

- "Cryptogeometric Sentinel wipes the party" -- Avoid splitting the aggro trigger; engage coherently from a defensive position.
- "Inquisitorial barrier blocks me" -- It only blocks Dogmatic/Iconoclast if you are Heretical, or vice versa. The barrier is conviction-gated, not locked.

## Soft-lock warnings

None beyond E14 (which is the game's final point of no return -- save before the C'tan console).

## Exit
