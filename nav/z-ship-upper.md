## Entry

Via section-map from any voidship zone (z-ship-bridge, z-ship-lower, z-ship-cargo, z-ship-quarters). Always accessible.

## Entry tips

1. The **FURIA plate altar** (Voidship Shrine) is an optional puzzle with a permanent reward -- complete it early Ch.1; plate-placement order is in the Sequential gates list below.
2. **Navis Sanctum** (Cassia's quarters) unlocks after Ch.1; her companion quest windows open here.
3. **Janris's office** contains Defective Servitors quest elements -- revisit when that quest chain flags new objectives.

## Sequential gates

1. **FURIA plate altar** -- Press button to raise five plates; place party members on F-U-R-I-A positions (community-mapped). Reward behind the altar. This puzzle is documented here rather than in its own `puzzles/` file.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none (can retry if wrong configuration)
2. **Astropathic Choir** -- Chapter-keyed events; consult for incoming-message updates.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
3. **Navis Sanctum** -- Cassia's companion area (unlocks post-Ch.1). Companion quest The Price of Power starts here in late Ch.4.
   - `point_of_no_return:` none
4. **Janris's office** -- Defective Servitors quest node. Required for "How Did It All End?" achievement chain.
   - `point_of_no_return:` missable-trigger (interact before Dargonus inauguration for Ch.1-2 elements)

## Optional branches

- **FURIA puzzle** -- Optional but permanent reward; accessible any time from Ch.1 onward.

## Common confusions

- Janris's office is on Upper Deck, not the Bridge; players sometimes look for him only on the Bridge.

None confirmed.
