## Known Control Issues / Gotchas

- **Space (End Turn) is hard-coded.** Cannot be remapped in-game without the Enhanced Controls Nexus mod (#14).
- **Formation toggle** has no default keybinding -- must use ESC menu every time.
- **C'tan final boss (Ch5) teleport-swap with clones:** use **Alt+1-6** to direct-select party members rather than clicking silhouettes (avoids mistargeting the clone).
- **Hidden objects (Ch1 dense layout):** hold Tab for object-highlight; Enhanced Controls mod adds passive (always-on) highlight.

## Recommended Remaps

- **Tab (Highlight):** bind to a thumb-mouse button in Footfall / dense NPC areas for hands-free scanning.
- **Enhanced Controls mod (Nexus Mods #14):** adds passive object-highlight, customizable hotkeys, forced-walk-while-Shift. Not on mainstream English guides but widely recommended by Russian StopGame.ru + Steam community.
- **Recharge Grenades mod (Nexus Mods #44):** quality-of-life fix for grenade economy; not a control remap.
- **Toy Box mod (Nexus Mods #1; open with Ctrl+F10 in-game):** 500+ cheats, tweaks, and QoL improvements. See `toybox.md` for full tab reference, risk tiers, and recommended settings. **First thing: enable "Enable Achievements with Mods installed" in Bag of Tricks or Steam achievements will not register.**

Controller and Steam Deck supported. Steam Deck launch flag `SteamDeck=0 %command%` forces keyboard+mouse UI (community workaround for Steam Deck's default controller-mode overlays).
