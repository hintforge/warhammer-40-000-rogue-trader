Imperial Inquisition. Allied by default; breaks on Heretical conviction.

## Political role

Heinrix's chain of command. Lord Inquisitor Calcazar's escort order in Ch.2 is the gating mechanic for Heinrix's permanent join. The Inquisition asks for Idira/Yrliet/Marazhai handover ("Holy Ordos" achievement).

## Chapters present

All chapters.

## Key figures

- **Lord Inquisitor Xavier Calcazar** -- Heinrix's superior; "Letter from Lord Inquisitor Calcazar" triggers permanent Heinrix join
- **Heinrix van Calox** -- Ordo Xenos operative; recruitable companion (see crew/heinrix-van-calox.md)

## Conviction interactions

Dogmatic-friendly. Heretical RT breaks the alliance; Heinrix leaves at Heretical Zealot capstone.
