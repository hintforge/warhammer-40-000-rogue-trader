Reawakened tomb-world threat. Primary Ch.5 enemy faction.

## Political role

Necron tomb-world at Epitaph; major Ch.5 antagonist. "Polyglot" achievement: learn the Necron language at Debris of Battiada cogitator (Ch.5) -- requires three knowledge checks; triggers Necron sentry battle immediately.

## Chapters present

Ch.4 (Epitaph approach), Ch.5 (primary).

## Key figures

- Necron Lord (Epitaph boss)
- Lokhust Heavy Destroyers (hardest unit type -- flanking required)
- Deathmarks

## Representative units

Necron Warrior, Blighted Immortal, Blighted Deathmark, Canoptek Scarab Swarm, Lokhust Heavy Destroyer, Cryptogeometric Sentinel, Elegy of Sorrow.

**Key mechanics:**
- Reanimation Protocol: auto-revive once per battle
- Living Metal (Destroyers): ignore ~95% damage unless flanked
- Scarabs: heal nearby Necron allies

## Conviction interactions

Combat-only faction; no direct conviction-gated dialogue tree. Inquisitorial barrier on z-epitaph is conviction-gated (Dogmatic/Iconoclast = passage; Heretical = destroy).
